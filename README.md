## 📗 DESCRIPTION

Melodial is a lightweight client-side mod that lets you instantly play any of Minecraft’s in-game music tracks at the press of a key. No jukeboxes, no waiting—just your soundtrack, your moment, anytime.

Whether you want to set the mood in creative mode, bring music to your builds, or relive nostalgic tracks on demand—it gives you the power to control the music of Minecraft, your way.

## ✨ KEY FEATURES

* Choose from the full library of Minecraft background music.
* Bind a key to cycle, play, or replay your favorite tracks.
* Enjoy full immersion with seamless, natural playback anytime.

## 🧩 USAGE

* Use the defined keys to play any music from the music list.

Open the config screen via Mod Menu or run the `/configure melodial` command to adjust features and tweaks.
